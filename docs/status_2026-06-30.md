**The sizing logic already accounts for them.** `ExecutionAgent.get_account_value()`

CMCSA sized to 40 shares = $984 ≈ 5% of the $20k sleeve, not 5% of $92k. So the ETFs
neither inflate nor starve the bot — freeing that capital would not change position
sizes at all. The whole `SIZING_NOTIONAL_CAP` mechanism exists because of this money.

is capped by `$20k × 5% = $1,000` by design, not by available capital ($274k buying
power, $12.6k cash sit idle relative to the cap).
