# DeepThinkTrader — Status 2026-06-25

## TL;DR

The council's step 2 — replay the closed trades at corrected size to get a clean
expectancy number — is done. The answer reframes the problem twice.

The sizing bug was a red herring for the *edge* question: R-multiple
(`pnl / risk_amount`) is sizing-independent, and the strategy is **still negative
at −0.147R/trade** across 243 clean trades. So the council's kill criterion is
triggered — the entry has no positive edge after removing the 6x sizing inflation
and the money-path noise.

But the bleed is not entry quality and not position size. It is **stop-loss
slippage**, and the two books fail for *opposite*, both-fatal reasons:

- **Penny** has a real entry edge that illiquidity destroys at the exit.
- **Main** has honorable stops but no entry edge — its winners are cut too small.

## The pivotal number (sizing-independent)

R-multiple normalizes away position size entirely, so the 6x equity-inflation bug
cannot hide in it. Clean subset = 243 closed trades, excluding `alpaca_reconcile*`
and `cleanup_ghost*` bookkeeping exits.

| Cut | n | mean R | win % | avg win R | avg loss R |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| All closed | 293 | −0.136 | 54.3 | 0.71 | −1.24 |
| Clean | 243 | −0.147 | 56.4 | 0.72 | −1.28 |

A 56% win rate with negative expectancy means the loss side is too big — exactly
as the council's arithmetic predicted. The R-multiple shows *why*: losers average
−1.28R when a stop is supposed to cap a loss at −1.0R.

## Where the loss actually is: stops blow through their level

By exit mechanism (clean subset), everything except the stop-loss bucket is
positive:

| Mechanism | n | mean R | total R |
|---|---|---|---|
| trailing | 132 | +0.49 | +64.1 |
| take_profit | 17 | +1.62 | +27.6 |
| time_stop | 2 | +0.18 | +0.4 |
| earnings | 3 | +0.13 | +0.4 |
| **stop_loss** | **89** | **−1.44** | **−128.2** |

Stops average **−1.44R**, not −1.0R — they execute 44% past their level. That
excess (−0.44R × 89 stops ≈ −39R) is the entire shortfall.

**Counterfactual — if every stop were honored at exactly −1.0R:**
total R goes from −35.8 to **+3.4** (mean −0.147R → +0.014R). The whole negative
expectancy is stop slippage. Honored stops → break-even (not an edge, but not a
bleed).

## The two books fail for opposite reasons

Per-book, actual vs. the stops-honored-at-−1R counterfactual:

| Book | n | actual mean R | honored mean R | actual total R | honored total R |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| main | 74 | −0.205 | −0.134 | −15.2 | −9.9 |
| penny | 169 | −0.122 | **+0.079** | −20.6 | **+13.3** |

- **Penny: real edge, unexecutable.** With honored stops penny is *positive*
  (+0.079R). But honored stops are exactly what illiquidity denies: 44 of 58 penny
  stops blew past their level (worst −4.12R, −4.0R, −3.54R, all sub-$5 names). You
  cannot reliably stop out of an illiquid penny — it gaps through. The edge is real
  and structurally uncapturable. This is 86% of the total slippage leak.
- **Main: executable, no edge.** Main stops are mostly honored (−1.17R), but main
  is *still* negative even with perfect stops (−0.134R). Cause is the win side:
  main winners exit via trailing at only +0.40R (34 trades) and take-profit at
  +1.38R (just 5). Winners are cut too early to pay for −1R losers at a 54% hit
  rate.

## Verdict against the resume gate

- Gate 2 (passes the corrected validator): **failed.** Clean expectancy is
  −0.147R; neither book clears 1.0 in any honest cut.
- The 6x sizing bug (status_2026-06-23): real, but it is a **dollar-risk** problem,
  not an edge problem. It does not appear in R-space. Fixing it (account split or

## What a viable bot would require (not yet built)

The infrastructure is sound and reusable — risk engine, 5-min exit manager,
dashboard, reflection loop, and the `backtest/` walk-forward + signal-search
harness all work. What's missing is a signal with both properties at once:

1. **On liquid names** (so stops are honorable — penny's killer is removed), and
2. **With winners large enough** to pay for −1R losers at the achievable hit rate
   (main's killer is removed).

That is the offline `signal_search.py` / `walk_forward_entries.py` job — run it on
liquid universes, rank by bootstrap PF CI, and only a class that clears 1.0 offline
earns a live slot. This is the "weeks of work," now properly motivated by the clean
number rather than started on hope.

## Signal search (council step 3) — a validated lead on liquid names

Ran `backtest.signal_search` (re-scores 8 candidate rules from raw research
reports, simulates forward net of cost, reports PF per time-third) plus a
5,000-sample bootstrap PF 95% CI on the MAIN (≥$5, liquid) book. Gate = CI lower
bound > 1.0.

| Signal | n | win% | PF | bootstrap 95% CI [lo, med, hi] | gate |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| momentum_breakout | 280 | 48.9 | 2.78 | [1.67, 2.74, 4.22] | **PASS** |
| relative_strength | 443 | 51.5 | 2.38 | [1.57, 2.35, 3.46] | **PASS** |
| quality_momentum *(current live)* | 293 | 59.7 | 1.14 | [0.84, 1.14, 1.54] | fail |
| SA_bull_skew | 531 | 55.0 | 1.48 | [1.02, 1.45, 2.23] | pass (thin) |
| 1h_trend_align | 257 | 53.3 | 1.53 | [1.08, 1.53, 2.13] | pass (thin) |

Key reads:
- **The current live main signal (`quality_momentum`) FAILS the gate** (CI lower
  0.84) — the in-sample confirmation of the live −0.13R bleed. The bot was running
  a no-edge rule.
- **`momentum_breakout` and `relative_strength` clear the gate with margin**
  (lower bounds 1.67 / 1.57), on liquid names, with PF>1 in all three time-thirds
  (momentum strongest in the most recent third: 4.49). These are real leads.
- Penny PF figures (12+) are illiquidity artifacts (per-thirds collapse 49→15→3,
  34% win) and discarded — consistent with the unexecutable-stops finding above.

**Caveats (do not over-read):** this is in-sample over one ~3-month window
(Mar–Jun 2026). The bootstrap controls for a lucky-few-trades fluke, not a lucky
*regime* — and momentum is the most regime-sensitive signal class there is. The
per-third consistency is a partial OOS check that held, but the only honest OOS
test is a true forward run. So these earn a **live tiny-notional paper forward
test**, not live capital.
